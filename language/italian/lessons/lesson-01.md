# Lesson 01 — Ciao! (greetings + pronunciation + introducing yourself)

## Goal

By the end of this lesson you can:
- say hello/goodbye politely
- introduce yourself: “My name is…”
- ask someone’s name and how they are

## Pronunciation for today’s words (beginner-friendly)

Notes:
- The **bold** syllable is the stress (what you say a little louder/longer).
- The “approx” is an English-friendly hint, not perfect.

- **ciao** — *cià-o* — approx “chow”
- **buongiorno** — *buon-**GIOR**-no* — approx “bwon-JOR-no”
- **buonasera** — *buo-na-**SE**-ra* — approx “bwoh-na-SEH-ra”
- **arrivederci** — *ar-ri-ve-**DER**-ci* — approx “ah-ree-veh-DER-chee”
- **per favore** — *per fa-**VO**-re* — approx “pehr fah-VOH-reh”
- **grazie** — ***GRA**-tsie* — approx “GRAH-tsee-eh”
- **prego** — ***PRE**-go* — approx “PREH-go”
- **scusa** — ***SCU**-sa* — approx “SKOO-za”
- **scusi** — ***SCU**-si* — approx “SKOO-zee”
- **mi chiamo** — *mi **KIA**-mo* — approx “mee KYAH-moh”
- **piacere** — *pia-**CE**-re* — approx “pya-CHEH-reh”
- **come** — ***CO**-me* — approx “KOH-meh”
- **come stai?** — ***CO**-me stai?* — approx “KOH-meh stye?”
- **bene** — ***BE**-ne* — approx “BEH-neh”
- **così così** — *co-**SÌ** co-**SÌ*** — approx “koh-SEE koh-SEE”
- **male** — ***MA**-le* — approx “MAH-leh”

## Pronunciation: quick essentials (read out loud)

- **C**:
  - **ca, co, cu** = “k” sound: *casa, come, cultura*
  - **ce, ci** = “ch” sound: *cena, ciao*
  - **che, chi** = “k” sound: *che, chi*
- **G**:
  - **ga, go, gu** = “g” (as in “go”): *gatto, gomma*
  - **ge, gi** = soft “j” sound: *gelato, giro*
  - **ghe, ghi** = hard “g”: *spaghetti, ghiaccio*
- **H** is silent: *ho, hotel* (often borrowed words)
- **GN** ≈ “ny”: *Italia* has none, but *lasagna* does: *la-sa-gnà*
- **GLI** is special: *famiglia* (don’t worry—practice slowly)

## Core phrases

- **Ciao** — Hi / Bye (informal)
- **Buongiorno** — Good morning / Good day (polite)
- **Buonasera** — Good evening (polite)
- **Arrivederci** — Goodbye (polite)
- **Per favore** — Please
- **Grazie** — Thank you
- **Prego** — You’re welcome / please (when offering)
- **Scusa** — Sorry / excuse me (informal, to a friend)
- **Scusi** — Sorry / excuse me (polite, to a stranger)

## Introducing yourself

- **Mi chiamo …** — My name is …
- **Io sono …** — I am … (name / nationality / profession)
- **Piacere!** — Nice to meet you!

## Asking questions (starter set)

- **Come ti chiami?** — What’s your name? (informal)
- **Come si chiama?** — What’s your name? (polite)
- **Come stai?** — How are you? (informal)
- **Come sta?** — How are you? (polite)

## Mini-dialogue (say it out loud)

**A:** Ciao! Come ti chiami?  
**B:** Ciao! Mi chiamo Marco. E tu?  
**A:** Mi chiamo Anna. Piacere!  
**B:** Piacere! Come stai?  
**A:** Bene, grazie. E tu?  
**B:** Così così.

Useful answers:
- **Bene** — well / fine
- **Così così** — so-so
- **Male** — bad

## Exercises

### A) Fill in the blanks

1) ____! (polite “goodbye”)  
2) Mi ____ Anna.  
3) ____ stai?  
4) Bene, ____.  
5) ____, a friend: “excuse me / sorry”

### B) Choose the polite version

Write **(I)** for informal or **(P)** for polite.

1) Come **ti** chiami?  
2) Come **si** chiama?  
3) Come **stai**?  
4) Come **sta**?

### C) Speak (30 seconds)

Say out loud (don’t translate, just speak Italian):
- Hello (polite), my name is ____. Nice to meet you. I’m fine, thank you.
