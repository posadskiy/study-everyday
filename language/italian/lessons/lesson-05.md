# Lesson 05 — At the café (ordering + polite requests with “vorrei”)

## Goal

By the end of this lesson you can:
- order coffee/food politely
- ask the price and ask for the bill
- use **vorrei** (“I would like”) for polite requests

## Pronunciation for today’s words

Notes:
- The **bold** syllable is the stress.
- “approx” is an English-friendly hint.

### Drinks & food

- **caffè** — caf-**FÈ** — approx “kah-FEH” (stress on the end)
- **cappuccino** — cap-puc-**CI**-no — approx “kap-poo-CHEE-noh”
- **tè** — **TÈ** — approx “teh” (short)
- **acqua** — **AC**-qua — approx “AHK-kwah”
- **panino** — pa-**NI**-no — approx “pah-NEE-noh”
- **brioche** — bri-**O**-sh — approx “bree-OHSH” (Italian often says it like “bri-OSH”)

### Useful phrases

- **vorrei** — vor-**REI** — approx “vohr-RAY”
- **per favore** — per fa-**VO**-re — approx “pehr fah-VOH-reh”
- **grazie** — **GRA**-tsie — approx “GRAH-tsee-eh”
- **prego** — **PRE**-go — approx “PREH-go”
- **quanto costa?** — **QUAN**-to **COS**-ta? — approx “KWAHN-toh KOS-tah?”
- **il conto** — il **CON**-to — approx “eel KON-toh”
- **subito** — **SU**-bi-to — approx “SOO-bee-toh”
- **cliente** — cli-**EN**-te — approx “klee-EN-teh”
- **barista** — ba-**RIS**-ta — approx “bah-REES-tah”

## 1) Key café words

- **un caffè** — an espresso
- **un cappuccino** — a cappuccino
- **un tè** — tea
- **un’acqua** — water
- **un panino** — sandwich
- **una brioche** — croissant / sweet pastry
- **il conto** — the bill

## 2) Polite requests

- **Vorrei …** — I would like …
- **Per favore** — please
- **Grazie** — thank you

Examples:
- **Vorrei un caffè, per favore.**
- **Vorrei un’acqua.**

## 3) Asking the price

- **Quanto costa?** — How much is it?
- **Quanto costa un caffè?** — How much is a coffee?

## 4) Asking for the bill

- **Il conto, per favore.**

## Mini-dialogue (say it out loud)

**Cliente:** Buongiorno.  
**Barista:** Buongiorno.  
**Cliente:** Vorrei un cappuccino e una brioche, per favore.  
**Barista:** Subito.  
**Cliente:** Grazie.  
**Barista:** Prego.

Useful:
- **Subito** — right away
