# Month 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)

> Goal: introduce running impact safely. The body needs to learn this is happening at all before it can adapt to more.

## Primary Adaptations This Month

| Tissue | What's happening |
|--------|-----------------|
| Cardiovascular | Capillary growth, mitochondrial density (your hiking base helps here) |
| Calf muscle (soleus + gastrocnemius) | First adaptation to repeated impact loading |
| Neural pattern | Stride mechanics get more automatic, less wasteful |
| Tendons | Earliest signal — collagen synthesis stimulus, but no measurable stiffness gain yet |
| Bone | No measurable change yet — but the stimulus is being registered |

This is **only the beginning** of adaptation for everything below muscle. Treat it that way.

3 runs per week. 2 strength sessions. **3 full rest days.** Daily mobility is the same 10-min routine every day — see [`../strength/mobility.md`](../strength/mobility.md). On Tue/Thu it's most efficient right after the strength session.

## Week-by-Week Progression

| Week | Tue easy | Thu easy | Sat long | Total | Notes |
|------|---------|---------|---------|-------|-------|
| 1 | 30 min | 30 min | 50 min | ~110 min | Establish baseline. Stop if anything hurts. |
| 2 | 35 min | 35 min | 60 min | ~130 min | Same routes, slightly longer. |
| 3 | 40 min | 35 min | 70 min | ~145 min | Long run can include gentle rolling terrain. |
| 4 | **Deload** 30 min | **Deload** 25 min | **Deload** 50 min | ~105 min | Drop volume ~30%. This is when adaptation happens. |

**Approximate distance:** ~10–18 km/week. Don't track to km — track to time. On Karst terrain, km is meaningless.

## Run-Specific Rules — Month 1

### Pace and effort
- **All runs RPE 3–4.** Conversational pace. Full sentences possible while running.
- Heart rate Z1–Z2 (below ~150 bpm).
- If breathing becomes hard, walk. Resume running when breath returns. This is correct pacing, not failure.

### Terrain
- Flat to gently rolling only. No technical trail yet (ankles aren't ready for unpredictable foot placement).
- Karst limestone paths around Sežana are good. Save the proper trails for month 2+.
- Avoid hard pavement for more than 50% of any run — softer surfaces protect calves and shins in the highest-risk window.

### Cadence
- Target 170+ steps per minute. Count steps over 30 seconds × 2.
- If you're below 165, **deliberately shorten your stride** (don't reach forward with the foot). This is the single biggest reduction in impact per step.

### Walk breaks
- Plan 30 sec walk every 5 min on long run from week 1.
- On weekday runs, walk breaks only as needed, not scheduled.

## Strength This Month

Reference: [`../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md`](../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md) for full exercise descriptions.

**Volume for Month 1: bodyweight only, 2 sets per exercise, focus on movement quality.**

### Session A — Tuesday (25 min, strength only)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Note |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Single-leg calf raise (straight knee) | 2 × 10 each side | Slow: 3 s up, 3 s down. Off a step for full range. |
| Bulgarian split squat (bodyweight) | 2 × 8 each side | Slow eccentric, no weight. |
| Step-down | 2 × 8 each side | 20–30 cm step. Control descent. |
| Dead bug | 2 × 8 each side | Lower back flat to floor. |

### Session B — Thursday (25 min, strength only)

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Note |
|---------|-------------|------|
| Hip thrust (bodyweight) | 2 × 12 | 1-sec squeeze at top. |
| Single-leg squat to box | 2 × 6 each side | Box version only — don't progress yet. |
| Clamshells | 2 × 12 each side | No band yet. |
| Tibialis raise | 2 × 15 | Crucial — calves need their antagonist trained. |
| Side plank | 2 × 20 sec each side | Build to 30 sec by end of month. |

**Form is the entire point this month.** Stop a set if form breaks. Watch the Squat University and James Dunne YouTube videos linked in `../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md` if any exercise feels uncertain.

### Daily mobility (10 min, every day — separate from strength)

The full routine lives in [`../strength/mobility.md`](../strength/mobility.md). It is the **same 10-min routine every day**, done once per day:

1. Hip flexor stretch — 60 sec each side
2. Calf stretch (straight knee + bent knee) — 30 sec each, both sides
3. Ankle dorsiflexion (knee-to-wall) — ~1 min per foot
4. Hamstring stretch — 60 sec each side
5. Figure-4 / IT band — 60 sec each side
6. Thoracic rotation (thread the needle) — 10 reps each side

**When to do it:** Tue and Thu → immediately after strength as cool-down. Mon/Wed/Fri/Sun → any time (morning, before bed). Sat → after the long run.

> **Do not add daily calf raises or tibialis raises on top.** Calves get heavy work in Session A. Tibialis gets work in Session B. Both also load on every run. Adding more daily is how the calf-clog issue gets triggered.

## Nutrition This Month

You're in weight loss phase. See [`../nutrition/nutrition.md`](../nutrition/nutrition.md) for the base targets. Adjustments specifically for Month 1's low volume:

| Day | Target | Why |
|-----|--------|-----|
| Rest day (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun) | 1900–2100 kcal | Same as standard rest day. |
| Easy run day (Tue, Thu) | 2300–2500 kcal | Run is only 30–45 min — light fueling needs. |
| Long run day (Sat) | 3000–3200 kcal | Even though long run is shorter than later phases, **don't deficit**. |

**Specific to Month 1:**
- **Long runs under 75 min** — water only on the run. No gel needed.
- **Post-run snack within 30 min** every run, including Tuesday/Thursday. 30 g protein + 50 g carbs.
- **Don't skip protein** on rest days. Aim 2.0 g/kg even on Monday and Friday (160 g/day).
- **Collagen + Vit C:** 15 g hydrolyzed collagen + 50 mg vit C 30–60 min before strength sessions. Targets the tissues that take the longest to adapt.
- **No alcohol on Friday night.** Disrupts the sleep that precedes your hardest run.

## Monitoring — What to Watch in Month 1

The first month is when most injuries develop. Watch these specifically.

### Daily checks
- Resting HR (first thing in morning, in bed): note baseline by end of week 1; flag any +5 bpm spike
- Calves on waking: tight? sore? rate 0–10. Should be ≤ 3 most mornings
- Sleep quality: 1–5 scale

### Weekly check (Sunday)
- Did I complete all 3 runs and 2 strength sessions? If no, why?
- Any niggle that persisted into day 2 or 3?
- Was the Saturday long run a step up from the previous Saturday, or harder than it should have been?

### Red flags for Month 1
| Signal | Response |
|--------|----------|
| Calves "clogged" or pumped on a low-effort run | Walk for 10 min, return easier. If recurs, drop to 2 runs/week. |
| Shin pain (front of lower leg) | Rest 3 days. Check cadence (likely too low) + tibialis raises daily. |
| Achilles soreness lasting 24h+ after running | 3 days off. Heavy slow calf raises become priority. |
| Knee pain on stairs | Glute weakness — increase clamshells and hip thrusts; back off step-downs. |
| Foot pain anywhere | Stop. Walk only for 1 week. Don't run on foot pain — this is how stress fractures start. |

## Graduation Criteria — Move to Month 2

End of week 4, check:

- [ ] Completed all 12 planned runs (or repeated weeks if necessary)
- [ ] No persistent niggle (≥ 3 days)
- [ ] Saturday 70 min run was completable at conversational pace
- [ ] Resting HR stable or slightly lower than week 1
- [ ] Motivation still present
- [ ] Calf "clog" event did not recur

If any are missing → repeat week 3 + week 4 once more before moving on. There's no penalty for an extra month here; the cost of moving on too fast is high.
