# Months 2–3 — Base Building (Weeks 5–12)

> Goal: extend aerobic endurance, introduce trail terrain gradually, begin neuromuscular speed work without intensity. The body that finished Month 1 is ready for *more*, not for *harder*.

## Primary Adaptations These Months

| Tissue | What's happening |
|--------|-----------------|
| Tendon stiffness (Achilles, patellar) | First measurable gains in stiffness — the basis of efficient running |
| Fascia (plantar, IT band) | Adapting to multidirectional load from trail terrain |
| Capillary network + mitochondria | Continuing to densify, especially around the slow-twitch fibers |
| Running economy (neural) | Stride becomes more efficient; same effort → slightly faster pace |
| Calf muscle | Bigger, more durable, less prone to "clog" feelings |
| Bone | Cortical remodeling stimulus increasing — no measurable change yet, but it's accumulating |

## Strides — Introduced Week 7

Strides are short relaxed accelerations, not sprints. They train running mechanics and recruit fast-twitch fibers without aerobic stress.

**How to do them:**
- Last 5 min of an otherwise easy run, on flat ground
- Accelerate over 5 seconds to ~85% effort (fast, but smooth and relaxed)
- Hold for 15–20 seconds
- Decelerate gradually
- Walk or easy jog 60 seconds full recovery
- Repeat 4–5 times

**They should feel:** good. Springy. Light. If they feel like a hard interval, you're going too hard — these are about mechanics, not effort.

**Where:** flat dirt path or quiet road. Not on technical trail, not on a steep hill.

## Run-Specific Rules — Months 2–3

### Pace and effort
- All runs RPE 3–4 except strides (RPE 6–7 for 15–20 seconds each).
- Heart rate Z1–Z2.
- Talk test applies — full sentences.

### Terrain — month 2 vs 3

**Month 2 (weeks 5–8):**
- Flat to gently rolling. Max 200 m D+ on long run.
- Soft surfaces preferred — dirt paths, easy Karst gravel.
- No technical trail yet.

**Month 3 (weeks 9–12):**
- Add real Karst trail to Saturday. Up to 500 m D+ by week 11.
- Tue and Thu runs stay easier terrain.
- **Hike steep uphills (> 8% grade or anything that elevates RPE above 4)**. This is correct technique, not a downgrade.
- **Descend carefully.** Walk technical descents in month 2; jog easy descents in month 3 with short controlled steps.

### Cadence
- Continue targeting 170+. Check once per run.
- On uphills, cadence stays high — stride length shortens.

## Nutrition — Months 2–3

Still in weight loss phase. The long run begins crossing 90 min by month 3, which changes fueling.

### Calorie targets

| Day | Month 2 | Month 3 | Why |
|-----|---------|---------|-----|
| Rest day (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun) | 1900–2100 kcal | 1900–2100 kcal | Standard deficit. |
| Easy run day (Tue, Thu) | 2400–2600 kcal | 2500–2700 kcal | Slightly more as runs lengthen. |
| Long run day (Sat) | 3100–3300 kcal | 3300–3500 kcal | No deficit. Recovery > weight loss. |

### Long run fueling — new this phase

| Duration | Carry | What to do |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Under 75 min (weeks 5–7) | Bladder plain water | Nothing — water only |
| 75–90 min (weeks 6–8) | Bladder + 1 flask light honey (15 g) | Sip flask from 40 min, chase with plain water |
| 90–110 min (weeks 9–11) | Bladder + 1 flask medium honey (30 g) + 4–5 dates | Sip every 25–30 min; eat 1–2 dates around 80 min |

**Flask recipe for this phase (light, weeks 6–8):** 250 ml water + 15 g honey (1 tbsp) + 1/8 tsp salt + squeeze of lemon.

**Flask recipe for this phase (medium, weeks 9–11):** 250 ml water + 30 g honey (2 tbsp) + 1/8 tsp salt + squeeze of lemon. Always chase each sip with plain water from bladder.

**Gut training rule:** start at 30 g carbs/hour. Gut absorbs carbs better over months — train it now on every long run that exceeds 75 min.

### Hydration on long runs
- Bladder (1 L plain water) is default carry from week 6 onward.
- Karst summer (Jun–Aug, > 28°C): shift Saturday run to 7 am. See hydration section in [`../nutrition/nutrition.md`](../nutrition/nutrition.md).

### Continuing
- 15 g collagen + 50 mg vit C, 30–60 min before strength sessions.
- Post-run protein within 30 min every run.
- Caffeine cutoff 14:00.

## Monitoring — Months 2–3

Same daily 1-minute check as Month 1. Add these phase-specific watch items:

### Weekly check (Sunday evening)
- Did weekly volume increase ≤ 12% from previous non-deload week?
- Long run cadence: average ≥ 168 spm? (Watch will show this)
- Did Saturday long run leave me wrecked or rested by Sunday evening?
- Strides: did they feel smooth, or were they forced?

### Red flags — Months 2–3

| Signal | Response |
|--------|----------|
| Achilles soreness > 24 h after run | 3 days off. Drop strides for 1 week. Focus on heavy slow calf work. |
| Knee pain (front) on descents | Check [Bulgarian split squat](../strength/lower-body/bulgarian-split-squat.md) tempo — too fast? Slow it. Eccentric quads need more work. |
| Knee pain (outer side) — IT band | [Lateral band walks](../strength/lower-body/lateral-band-walks.md) + [clamshells](../strength/lower-body/clamshells.md) daily. Don't ignore. |
| Plantar fascia (foot arch) pain in the morning | Reduce volume 30%. Roll arch with frozen bottle 5 min daily. [Tibialis raises](../strength/calves/tibialis-raise.md) continue. |
| Persistent fatigue 3 days running | Eating too little for the load. Increase carbs on training days by 50 g. |
| Sleep dropping below 7 h for 3+ nights | Cut the next planned increase. Hold volume flat for a week. |

## Graduation Criteria — Move to Month 4

End of week 12, check:

- [ ] Completed both Month 2 and Month 3 (with at most 1 missed run/week)
- [ ] Week 11 long run (110 min, 500 m D+) completed at conversational pace
- [ ] No persistent niggle in the last 6 weeks
- [ ] Strides feel smooth and springy, not forced
- [ ] Resting HR stable
- [ ] Saturday recovery complete by Sunday evening
- [ ] Comfortable running on basic Karst trail terrain

Month 4 introduces the first proper "quality" work (hill hike-jog intervals). The body must be solidly in Z2 endurance before adding intensity. If any criterion is missing, repeat weeks 10–12 once before progressing.
