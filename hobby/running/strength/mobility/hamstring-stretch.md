# Hamstring Stretch

Part of the [daily mobility routine](README.md). See `../../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md` for program context.

**What it trains:** hamstring length — tight hamstrings limit stride and increase lower back stress.

## How to do it (lying version — more controlled)
1. Lie on your back
2. Loop a towel or strap around one foot
3. Straighten the leg and gently pull it toward you until you feel tension in the back of the thigh
4. Keep the opposite leg flat on the floor
5. Hold 60 seconds per side, breathing through the stretch

**Seated version:** sit on the floor, one leg extended, one bent. Fold gently forward over the extended leg. Keep your back flat (hinge from the hips, do not round the spine).

## Common mistakes
- Rounding the lower back to reach further — this stretches the spine, not the hamstring; hinge from the hips
- Bending the knee to reduce tension — keep the leg as straight as comfortable
- Holding only 20–30 seconds — too short for tissue change

## Easier
- Keep a slight bend in the knee
- Use a towel or strap (reduces the grip effort)
- Seated version on a chair rather than the floor

## Harder
- Increase angle (pull leg closer to vertical)
- Perform standing single-leg forward fold — balance challenge added
- Add ankle dorsiflexion (flex the foot toward you while holding the stretch) to intensify
