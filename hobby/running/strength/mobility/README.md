# Mobility — Daily Routine

> This is your **single daily mobility routine**, 10 min, every day including rest days. Done once per day, not as part of strength sessions. On Tue/Thu it's most efficient as a cool-down right after strength. On other days do it any time — morning, before bed, or after a run.

Strength sessions (Sessions A and B) do not include mobility work. The calf raises and tibialis raises that protect calves and shins are inside the strength sessions on Tue and Thu — do not also add them daily. Mobility = stretches and joint range; strength = loading.

## The 10-min routine at a glance

| Order | Exercise | Time | Why |
|-------|----------|------|-----|
| 1 | [Hip flexor stretch](hip-flexor-stretch.md) | 60 sec × 2 sides = 2 min | Trail running keeps hips flexed for hours |
| 2 | [Calf stretch](calf-stretch.md) (straight knee + bent knee) | 30 sec × 2 each side = 2 min | Calves are the #1 stress site for beginners |
| 3 | [Ankle dorsiflexion](ankle-dorsiflexion.md) (knee-to-wall) | ~1 min per foot = 2 min | Controls knee tracking, prevents shin pain |
| 4 | [Hamstring stretch](hamstring-stretch.md) | 60 sec × 2 sides = 2 min | Stride length, lower back protection |
| 5 | [IT band / figure-4](figure-4-it-band.md) | 60 sec × 2 sides = 2 min | Lateral hip — common trail injury site |
| 6 | [Thoracic rotation](thoracic-rotation.md) (thread the needle) | 10 reps each side = ~1 min | Arm swing, balance, lower-back relief |

Total: ~11 min. Run through it in order. Read each exercise file once, then this table is enough.

Priority order: hip flexors → ankles → hamstrings → IT band → thoracic spine.
