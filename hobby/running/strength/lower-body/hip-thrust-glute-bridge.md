# Hip Thrust / Glute Bridge

**What it trains:** gluteus maximus — primary hip extensor, essential for uphill power and knee stability.

## How to do it (hip thrust — bench version)
1. Sit on the floor with upper back against a bench, knees bent, feet flat on floor hip-width apart
2. Drive hips up through heels until body forms a straight line from knees to shoulders
3. Squeeze glutes hard at the top — hold 1 second
4. Lower slowly (2–3 seconds)

**Glute bridge (floor version):** same movement but lying flat on the floor without the bench. Easier starting point.

## Common mistakes
- Arching the lower back at the top instead of squeezing glutes — the movement should come from glutes, not spine
- Feet too close to body — creates knee-dominant movement; move feet slightly further away
- Not completing full hip extension at the top

## Easier (beginner)
- Glute bridge on the floor
- Both feet on floor (bilateral)
- No weight

## Harder (progression)
- Single-leg version (one foot flat, other leg raised)
- Add barbell or dumbbell across hips
- Pause 3 seconds at the top
- Feet on an elevated surface (feet-elevated hip thrust)
