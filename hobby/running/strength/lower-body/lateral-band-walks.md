# Lateral Band Walks

**What it trains:** gluteus medius and minimus — lateral hip stability and knee alignment during running.

## How to do it
1. Place a resistance band around ankles (or just above knees for easier version)
2. Slightly bend knees and hips (quarter-squat position)
3. Step sideways — lead foot steps out, then trailing foot follows — maintaining band tension throughout
4. Keep toes pointing forward, do not let the trailing foot drag inward
5. Walk 15 steps one direction, then 15 back

## Common mistakes
- Standing upright — the partial squat is what loads the glutes
- Letting the band go slack between steps
- Feet turning outward — keep toes forward throughout
- Bouncing up and down with each step — stay at constant height

## Easier (beginner)
- Band above the knees (less resistance than at ankles)
- Smaller steps
- Stand more upright

## Harder (progression)
- Band at ankles with a stronger resistance band
- Add a squat between each step
- Perform with a slight forward lean (more hip-dominant)
