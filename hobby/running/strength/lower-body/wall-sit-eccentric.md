# Wall Sit with Eccentric

Used in: **Session A (Tuesday)** and **Session B (Thursday)**. See `../../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md` for full program.

**What it trains:** isometric quad endurance + eccentric control transitioning to single-leg.

## How to do it
1. Stand with back flat against the wall, feet ~60 cm from wall
2. Slide down until knees are at 90 degrees (thighs parallel to floor)
3. Hold for 30–45 seconds, breathing steadily
4. After the hold: shift weight to one leg, extend the other slightly, and slowly stand up on one leg over 4–5 seconds

## Common mistakes
- Knees falling inward during the hold — press them outward
- Holding breath — breathe through it
- Sliding feet back to make it easier — keep shins vertical or slightly angled

## Easier (beginner)
- Hold only for 15–20 seconds
- Skip the single-leg stand-up at the end
- Do not go to full 90 degrees — a shallower angle reduces load

## Harder (progression)
- Hold 60+ seconds
- Do the entire exercise on one leg (single-leg wall sit)
- Add a weight plate on the thighs
