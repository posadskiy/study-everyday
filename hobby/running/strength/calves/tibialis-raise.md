# Tibialis Anterior Raise

**What it trains:** tibialis anterior (front of the shin). Prevents shin splints and supports ankle dorsiflexion during the foot strike.

## How to do it
1. Stand with your back flat against a wall, heels about 30 cm away from the wall
2. Lift toes and the front of your foot as high as possible toward your shins
3. Lower slowly — full range down
4. Keep heels on the floor throughout
5. Both feet at the same time

## Common mistakes
- Heels too close to the wall — moves the exercise to the toes instead of the shin
- Partial range — lift as high as you can, the top range is where the muscle works hardest
- Going too fast — a controlled rhythm of about 1 second up, 2 seconds down is effective

## Easier (beginner)
- Seated version: sit on a chair, heels on floor, lift toes
- Reduce reps (start with 10–12)

## Harder (progression)
- Add a resistance band around the foot (looped around a fixed object)
- Single-leg version
- Perform on a slight incline to increase range at the top
