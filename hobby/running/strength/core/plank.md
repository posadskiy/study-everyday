# Plank

Used in: **Session A (Tuesday)** and **Session B (Thursday)**. See `../../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md` for full program.

**What it trains:** full core anti-extension stability, shoulder stability, glute and quad engagement.

## How to do it (standard forearm plank)
1. Forearms on the floor, elbows directly under shoulders
2. Body in a straight line from heels to head — no sagging at the hips, no raising the bum
3. Squeeze glutes and quads actively — the plank is not passive rest
4. Look at the floor about 30 cm in front of you (neutral neck)
5. Breathe steadily

## Common mistakes
- Hips sagging — if you cannot maintain a straight line, reduce hold time until you build strength
- Bum raised too high — this turns it into a rest position, not a plank
- Holding breath — breathe throughout
- Passive holding — actively squeeze glutes and brace the core, do not just hang in position

## Easier (beginner)
- Knees on the floor (reduces load by ~30%)
- Shorter holds (10–15 seconds)
- Standard plank before side plank

## Harder (progression)
- Extend hold time (60+ seconds)
- Add leg lift: in the plank position, lift one foot off the floor for 5 seconds, alternate
- Plank with shoulder tap: from high plank, alternate tapping each shoulder with the opposite hand — resist rotating the hips
