# Side Plank

Used in: **Session A (Tuesday)** and **Session B (Thursday)**. See `../../technique/06-strength-and-mobility.md` for full program.

**What it trains:** lateral core stability, shoulder stability, hip and oblique engagement.

## How to do it
1. Lie on your side, forearm on the floor, elbow under shoulder
2. Lift hips so body forms a straight line from feet to head
3. Keep hips stacked — do not let the top hip rotate forward or backward
4. Hold. Lower under control.

## Common mistakes
- Hips sagging — maintain a straight line from feet to head
- Top hip rotating forward or backward — keep hips stacked
- Holding breath — breathe throughout
- Passive holding — actively brace the core and squeeze glutes

## Easier (beginner)
- Knees on the floor (reduces load)
- Shorter holds (10–15 seconds)
- Master [plank.md](plank.md) first

## Harder (progression)
- Extend hold time (60+ seconds)
- Side plank with hip dip: lower hip toward floor and raise, 10–15 reps
- Add a top-leg lift or reach-through under the body
