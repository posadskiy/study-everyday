# Training Schedule

## Year Overview

```
Jan–Mar        Apr–May        Jun–Sep        Oct–Dec
BASE           BUILD          RACE           RECOVERY
Aerobic base   Add vertical   Race 1–4       Deload + reset
Strength focus Quality runs   Maintain       Strength focus
```

---

## Your Weekly Template (4 runs/week)

| Day | Session | Duration |
|-----|---------|----------|
| Mon | Rest | — |
| Tue | **Quality run** (hill repeats or tempo) + Strength A | 60 min + 35 min |
| Wed | Easy run | 45–55 min |
| Thu | Easy run + Strength B | 50 min + 35 min |
| Fri | Rest | — |
| Sat | **Long run** (trail, with vertical) | Phase-dependent |
| Sun | Rest | — |

**Quality run = hill repeats or sustained tempo climb.** Everything else = conversational pace. If you can't hold a short conversation, slow down.

---

## Phase Details

| Phase | Months | Weekly km | Weekly D+ | Sat long run | Saturday D+ |
|-------|--------|-----------|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Base | Jan–Mar | 30–45 km | 800–1500 m | 14–22 km | 700–1500 m |
| Build | Apr–May | 45–60 km | 1500–2800 m | 22–28 km | 1500–2500 m |
| Race | Jun–Sep | 40–55 km | 1200–2500 m | Race or 20–25 km | — |
| Recovery | Oct–Dec | 20–35 km | 400–900 m | 12–18 km, easy terrain | 400–700 m |

**Every 3rd or 4th week: cut volume by 35–40%.** Drop the quality session, shorten the long run. This is scheduled rest, not optional.

---

## Hill Repeats (Tuesday Quality Session)

Find a trail with 100–200m climb at 10–15% grade. Run up hard (breathing heavily but controlled), walk back down.

| Month | Repeats | Climb duration |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| 1–2 | 4 | 3 min |
| 3–4 | 5 | 4 min |
| 5–6 | 6 | 5 min |

---

## Race Week (Taper)

Cut volume 40–50% the week before. Keep 2–3 short quality efforts (5 min tempo × 2) to stay sharp. No new food, no new gear, no long hikes. Sleep priority.

## Post-Race Recovery

After any 30km+ race: minimum 7 days easy or off. Do not run hard again until you feel completely normal on stairs and easy walking.
