# Grape Cheat Sheet

Quick reference: grape → style → classic region → food pairing

---

## Red Grapes

| Grape | Body | Tannin | Acidity | Typical Aromas | Classic Region(s) | Best Food Pairing |
|-------|------|--------|---------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Cabernet Sauvignon | Full | High | High | Blackcurrant, cedar, bell pepper (unripe) | Bordeaux Left Bank, Napa Valley, Chile | Red meat, lamb, aged hard cheese |
| Merlot | Med–Full | Med | Med–High | Plum, cherry, chocolate | Pomerol, St-Émilion, California | Duck, pork, pasta with meat |
| Pinot Noir | Light–Med | Low | High | Red cherry, raspberry, earth, mushroom (aged) | Burgundy, Oregon, NZ | Salmon, duck, mushrooms, charcuterie |
| Syrah / Shiraz | Full | High | Med–High | Pepper/olive/smoke (cool) or dark jam/chocolate (warm) | N. Rhône, Barossa Valley | Grilled lamb, game, BBQ |
| Grenache / Garnacha | Med–Full | Low | Med | Raspberry, dried herbs, white pepper | S. Rhône, Priorat, Sardinia | Mediterranean food, lamb, tapas |
| Malbec | Full | Med–High | Med | Dark plum, violet, chocolate | Mendoza, Argentina | Steak, grilled meats |
| Tempranillo | Medium | Medium | Medium | Cherry, vanilla, leather (oak-aged) | Rioja, Ribera del Duero | Lamb, roast meats, manchego |
| Sangiovese | Med–Full | High | Very High | Sour cherry, tomato, dried herb | Chianti, Brunello, Montepulciano | All Italian food, especially tomato sauces |
| Nebbiolo | Full | Very High | Very High | Rose, tar, cherry, tobacco, leather (aged) | Barolo, Barbaresco | White truffle, braised game, aged Parmigiano |
| Zinfandel / Primitivo | Full | Med | Med–Low | Blackberry, dried fruit, pepper | Sonoma, Paso Robles; Puglia (IT) | BBQ, spicy food, pizza |

## White Grapes

| Grape | Body | Sweetness | Acidity | Typical Aromas | Classic Region(s) | Best Food Pairing |
|-------|------|-----------|---------|----------------|------------------|------------------|
| Chardonnay (unoaked) | Light–Med | Dry | High | Green apple, citrus, chalk | Chablis, Mâconnais | Oysters, light fish, seafood |
| Chardonnay (oaked) | Full | Dry | Med | Butter, vanilla, tropical fruit, toast | White Burgundy, California | Lobster, cream sauces, roast chicken |
| Sauvignon Blanc | Light–Med | Dry | High | Grapefruit, cut grass, gooseberry, nettles | Sancerre, Marlborough | Goat cheese, asparagus, grilled fish |
| Riesling (dry) | Light | Dry | Very High | Lime, slate, green apple, petrol (aged) | Mosel, Alsace, Wachau | Spicy food, pork, sushi |
| Riesling (sweet) | Light | Off-dry → Sweet | Very High | Apricot, peach, honey, petrol | Mosel Spätlese/Auslese | Foie gras, Asian food, blue cheese |
| Pinot Grigio (IT) | Light | Dry | High | Neutral, citrus, white peach | Friuli Isonzo, Alto Adige | Light seafood, pizza, aperitivo |
| Pinot Gris (Alsace) | Full | Off-dry | Med | Honey, spice, apricot | Alsace | Foie gras, pork, spiced food |
| Gewürztraminer | Full | Dry–Off-dry | Low | Rose, lychee, ginger, Turkish delight | Alsace, Alto Adige | Spicy Asian, foie gras, blue cheese |
| Viognier | Full | Dry | Low | Apricot, peach, orange blossom, perfume | Condrieu, Languedoc | Lobster, Thai curry, cream dishes |
| Chenin Blanc | Light–Full | Varies | High | Apple, quince, honey, wax (aged) | Loire (Vouvray, Savennières) | Matches sweetness level of wine to dish |
| Albariño / Alvarinho | Light–Med | Dry | High | Citrus, peach, saline | Rías Baixas, Vinho Verde | Seafood, octopus, grilled fish |

## FVG & Slovenian Native Grapes

| Grape | Color | Body | Typical Aromas | Region | Best Food Pairing |
|-------|-------|------|----------------|--------|------------------|
| Friulano (Jakot) | White | Med | Almond, hay, white peach, bitter finish | Collio, COF, Brda | Prosciutto, frico, light seafood |
| Ribolla Gialla / Rebula | White | Med (+ tannic as orange wine) | Citrus, apple; dried fruit/walnut as orange wine | Collio, Brda | Seafood (conventional); pork/cheese (orange) |
| Malvasia Istriana | White | Med | Apricot, floral, almond, gentle | Carso, Collio, Brda, Istra | Grilled seafood, risotto, light pasta |
| Vitovska | White | Med | Mineral, saline, flint, citrus, white flowers | Carso / Kras only | Oysters, raw fish, Karst cheese, seafood |
| Pinela | White | Light | Floral, lemon, chamomile, delicate | Vipava Valley only | Salads, poultry, fresh cheese, aperitivo |
| Zelen | White | Light | Fresh herbs, green apple, nettle | Vipava Valley only | Vegetables, grilled fish, herby pasta |
| Refosco d.P.R. / Teran | Red | Med–Full | Dark cherry, black olive, earth, bitter herb | COF, Carso, Kras | Brovada e muset, game, fatty sausages |
| Schioppettino | Red | Medium | Black pepper, wild berry, violet, wild herbs | COF (Prepotto) | Game, pheasant, mushrooms, aged cheese |
| Pignolo | Red | Full | Dense dark fruit, leather, chocolate, herbs | COF (rare) | Braised game, wild boar, aged hard cheese |
| Tazzelenghe | Red | Med | Sour cherry, high acid, earthy | COF (rare) | Prosciutto, fatty salumi, liver dishes |
| Modra Frankinja | Red | Med | Dark cherry, pepper, herbal, structured | Podravje, Burgenland | Goulash, pork, game |
