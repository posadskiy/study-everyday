# Vintage Chart

Quality ratings for key wine regions by year.

**Scale:** ★★★★★ exceptional | ★★★★ very good | ★★★ good | ★★ average | ★ difficult | — not yet assessed / not applicable

Use this when buying wine intended for aging, or when evaluating whether to pay a premium for a particular vintage.

> **Note:** Vintage ratings are generalizations across the region. Individual producers can over- or under-perform relative to the regional average. A skilled winemaker in a challenging vintage can still make an excellent wine.

## How to Use This Chart

1. **Identify the region** of the wine you're considering
2. **Check the vintage** against the rating
3. **Adjust your decision:** In a great vintage, even lesser producers often make fine wine. In a difficult vintage, you need to trust the producer more

**General principle:** For wines under €20, don't worry much about vintage. For wines €30+, or wines intended for aging, vintage matters significantly.

**Best currently available vintages for buying (2025–2026):**
- Barolo/Barbaresco: 2016, 2013 (drinking), 2019, 2021 (to cellar)
- Red Burgundy: 2015, 2019, 2020 (drinking); 2021, 2022 (to cellar)  
- White Burgundy: 2017, 2019, 2020
- Bordeaux: 2014, 2016 (drinking); 2018, 2019, 2020 (cellar)
- FVG & Slovenia: 2019, 2020, 2021 (all drinking well)
